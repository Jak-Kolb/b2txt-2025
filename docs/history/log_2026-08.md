## 2026-08-07 12:30 — C29 rescoring: 8.49 % -> 6.89 %, and a measured latency frontier

Shipped:
- `benchmark/neural_rescore.py` — n-best rescoring with any HF causal LM, sweeping gamma (4-gram
  weight), alpha (neural weight), beta (length), and top_k.

Learned:
- **Best: gpt2-large, gamma=0.25, alpha=1.0 -> 6.89 % val-dev (unseen 7.09 %), 29 % of the 5.62-pt
  oracle headroom.**
- **A scoring bug cost more than 6x the parameters.** Adding the neural score on top of a
  full-weight 4-gram double-counts the LM. Weighting the 4-gram by gamma fixed it: gpt2-small went
  7.83 -> 7.38, gpt2-large 7.58 -> 6.89. **Always sweep gamma** — pinned at 1 it makes model
  scaling look flat and the whole approach look dead. gamma=0 (full replacement) is worse than
  0.25: down-weight the n-gram, don't discard it.
- Scaling (gamma swept, UNSEEN WER): none 8.72 | gpt2 124M 7.63 | Qwen0.5B 7.54 | **gpt2-large
  774M 7.09** | Qwen1.5B 7.19. **Plateaus at ~29 % of headroom; scale stops paying past ~800M.**
  Qwen1.5B is worse than gpt2-large at 2x params and 2.5x latency (p95 190.9 ms, over the 140 ms
  budget). Parameters beat architecture modernity here.
- **Domain adaptation REJECTED, and at scale it HURTS.** Fine-tuning gpt2-large on the 8,072 TRAIN
  transcriptions cut held-out ppl **395.14 -> 27.44 (14x)** and made unseen WER **worse**:
  7.09 -> 7.42. On gpt2-small it moved unseen 0.05 pts while moving seen 1.04 (4.01 -> 2.97).
  Catastrophic forgetting: narrowing to the prompt distribution destroys the general knowledge
  doing the discrimination. **Fourth ppl/WER divergence, and the sharpest.**
- In-domain adaptation of the LM stage is now 4-for-4 negative on unseen data (interpolation,
  trigram rescoring, neural FT small, neural FT large). The 6.94 % verbatim overlap is what makes
  it look otherwise on any metric that doesn't split seen from unseen.
- Memory note: Qwen vocab is 151,936 vs GPT-2's 50,257, so a separate log_softmax copy OOMs 16 GB.
  Use fused cross_entropy (verified numerically identical: gpt2 reproduces 7.38 %).
- **Frontier** (total finalization p95 = 21.3 ms nbest extraction + neural forward):
  no rescore 8.49 %/6 ms | top-5 7.45 %/35 ms | top-10 7.35 %/37 ms | full ~30 6.89 %/96 ms.
  Oracle gain is spread THROUGH the list; 30->10 costs 9 pts of headroom. Model size and list depth
  are substitutable. All inside the 140 ms budget vs the shipped stack's 620-830 ms.
